Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you pick up the phone.

Do I own the website when it’s finished?

Yes. The finished site — domain connection, files, and content — is yours. Riverbed Designs does not lock you into a proprietary platform or claim ownership of your digital presence.

Do I have to manage anything technical myself?

No. You never log into a builder, never touch hosting settings, and never deal with updates through a dashboard. Changes are submitted by phone, text, or email and posted for you.

How do updates work after the site is live?

Hours, seasonal notices, inventory text, and similar operational changes are sent by email, call, or text. On care packages they are posted within 24–48 hours. Package 1 clients can request pay-as-you-go updates as needed.

How is pricing handled?

Scope and pricing are discussed directly by phone or email so the package matches your actual business needs. No public price list on the site — call or email for a clear quote.

How long does a typical build take?

Most Turnkey Signboard and managed-care builds are completed and live within a focused development window once content and direction are confirmed. Timeline is confirmed on the initial call.

What do I need to provide?

Business details, hours, services, and any photos or story points you already have. We handle structure, layout, copy refinement, technical deployment, and ongoing posting. You stay focused on the shop floor.
