Cancellation & Refund Policy

Operating Identity: Rebecca / Riverbed Designs (Deeply Rooted Enterprises) • Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview & Nature of Services

Riverbed Designs operates strictly as a bespoke professional services firm specializing in custom website architecture, digital asset creation, and ongoing platform maintenance. All services, creative work, written copy, photography, and code are delivered exclusively via electronic transmission. Because our offerings consist entirely of custom professional labor, time, and digital configurations tailored to individual client specifications, our refund and cancellation policies reflect the irrevocable nature of custom service delivery.

2. Custom Website Builds & Setup Fees (Packages 1–4)

  • Initial Deposits & Upfront Build Fees: For custom builds requiring a deposit (Package 1) or full upfront build fees (Packages 2–4), payments are required prior to project kickoff, research, drafting, or scheduling on-site photography.
  • Non-Refundable Once Work Commences: Once development, design scoping, copywriting, or on-site asset gathering has commenced, all setup fees and deposits are strictly non-refundable. This covers the administrative time, scheduling, and labor dedicated to the project.
  • Cancellation Prior to Commencement: If a client requests project cancellation in writing before any design, development, or on-site labor has begun, the upfront fee will be refunded minus a fifty-dollar ($50.00) administrative setup fee.
  • Balance Due Upon Launch: Final project balances (e.g., the remaining 50% on Package 1) are due prior to pointing the custom domain live and completing final handoff. Once the final balance is paid and the website is deployed to the client's live domain, no refunds will be granted under any circumstances.

3. Ongoing Maintenance Retainers (Annual & Monthly)

  • Monthly Maintenance Retainers: Monthly maintenance retainers are billed in advance on a recurring monthly cycle. Clients may cancel ongoing monthly service at any time by providing fourteen (14) days written notice (via email or text) prior to their next scheduled billing date. Monthly retainer fees already billed are non-refundable, and service remains active through the end of the paid billing period.
  • Annual Caretaker Retainers (Upfront Annual Maintenance): Annual maintenance plans are discounted packages paid upfront for twelve (12) consecutive months of priority updates and domain management. If an annual maintenance plan is terminated early by the client, no pro-rated refunds will be issued for unused months, as the annual rate reflects a discounted block of reserved service.
  • Domain Registrations & External Fees: Any third-party expenses, including annual domain registration renewals or registrar fees paid on the client's behalf, are completely non-refundable once registered with the domain naming authority.

4. Standalone Add-Ons & Photography

  • Pay-As-You-Go Edits: Individual text, hours, or announcement edits are billed per request and are non-refundable once published to the live site.
  • On-Site Product Photography: Fees for scheduled on-site photography sessions and product descriptions are earned upon completion of the photo session and are non-refundable once the media has been captured and edited.

5. Defects & Corrections (Service Satisfaction)

While financial refunds are not issued for completed labor, Riverbed Designs is dedicated to delivering accurate, functional web infrastructure:

  • 14-Day Post-Launch Review: Following live deployment, clients have fourteen (14) calendar days to report any technical bugs, broken navigation buttons, typographical errors, or inaccurate operational hours resulting from our build. Riverbed Designs will rectify these verified defects promptly at zero additional charge.
  • Scope Limitations: The post-launch review covers errors within the originally agreed project scope. Fundamental layout overhauls, requests for new features, or complete re-branding outside the signed agreement will be quoted as new work.

6. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

Clients agree to contact Riverbed Designs directly to resolve any billing inquiries or concerns prior to initiating a payment dispute or chargeback with their bank or credit card processor. In the event of an unjustified chargeback or payment reversal, Riverbed Designs reserves the right to immediately suspend hosting configurations, revoke third-party profile connections, and pursue collection of outstanding fees.
