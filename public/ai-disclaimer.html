AI Use & Assistive Technology Disclaimer

Operating Identity: Rebecca / Riverbed Designs (Deeply Rooted Enterprises) • Effective Date: September 2026

1. Purpose & Assistive Technology Disclosure

Riverbed Designs utilizes Google Gemini, along with related algorithmic software and code linting tools, as assistive aids during research, preliminary drafting, structure optimization, and technical troubleshooting. This disclosure outlines our principles regarding client data, human oversight, and work originality.

2. Direct Human Oversight & Validation

Every deliverable produced by Riverbed Designs—including website code, written site copy, graphic wayfinding assets, and profile configurations—is personally authored, reviewed, crafted, and verified by human hands. No website code or client-facing media is deployed autonomously by unmonitored artificial intelligence models. Google Gemini is utilized solely as a collaborative and analytical workbench to enhance production speed and accuracy.

3. Confidentiality & Proprietary Client Information

We respect client privacy. Riverbed Designs does not submit non-public client financial records, login credentials, trade secrets, or proprietary customer information into open-domain or public artificial intelligence model training pools.

4. Accuracy & Client Review Responsibility

While assistive technological tools streamline research and code organization, computational models may occasionally produce factual errors, outdated regional information, or hallucinations. Riverbed Designs verifies all technical deliverables; however, the client retains final review and approval responsibility for all published business details—including operating hours, pricing, product descriptions, and contact credentials—prior to live domain deployment.
