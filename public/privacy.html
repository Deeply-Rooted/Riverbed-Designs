1. Overview & Scope

Riverbed Designs ("we," "us," or "our"), an initiative of Deeply Rooted Enterprises, respects your privacy. 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide web design estimates, communicate regarding projects, and fulfill client contracts:

  • Contact Information: Your name, business name, physical address, email address, and telephone number when you contact us via email, phone, or text.
  • Project Details: Information provided through questionnaires, including business history, operating schedules, photos, and domain names.
  • Technical Data: Standard server logs from our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare), which may include IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-context tracking pixels or behavioral advertising cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To prepare customized proposals and deliver electronic web design labor.
  • To communicate regarding updates, emergency maintenance, and inquiries.
  • To process invoicing and billing securely through Stripe.
  • To configure technical infrastructure (such as domain registrars, DNS, and Google Business listings) authorized by contract.

4. Disclosure & Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. We disclose data only to trusted service providers performing essential functions under strict confidentiality: Stripe (payment processing), Cloudflare (edge hosting & security), and domain registrars/Google Business Profile (strictly to register and verify your web address).

5. California Privacy Disclosures (CalOPPA & CCPA)

  • Do Not Track: Our site does not track users over time across third-party websites to serve targeted ads, and operates consistently regardless of DNT browser signals.
  • No Third-Party Ad Networks: We do not permit external behavioral ad networks to harvest personally identifiable information from users on our site.
  • Zero Data Sales: We have not sold or shared any personal information in the preceding 12 months.
  • Consumer Rights: California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal records by contacting us directly.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain client records only as long as necessary to fulfill contracts and satisfy statutory tax and accounting retention requirements.
